# Privacy Policy for Gigaless

**Last Updated: August 30, 2026**

Gigaless ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

## 1. Zero Cloud Uploads (100% Local Processing)
Gigaless operates entirely on your device. When you grant photo library permissions:
* Your photos, videos, and metadata are scanned and processed locally on your phone.
* We do NOT upload, transfer, copy, or store your photos on any external servers or cloud services.
* We do NOT have access to your personal images.

## 2. Permissions We Request
* **Photo Library / Storage Access:** Required solely to identify screenshots, duplicate images, and large files on your device and to allow you to move unwanted files to your device's native Recently Deleted folder.

## 3. Advertising & Analytics
We may use trusted third-party advertising partners such as Google AdMob to deliver banner and interstitial advertisements. These partners may collect anonymized device identifiers and usage metrics in accordance with their respective privacy policies.
